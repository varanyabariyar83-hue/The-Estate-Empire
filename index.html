About the Clinic

Your Neighbourhood
Doctor, Always There


Dr. Intjar Khan Clinic is a trusted general medical practice in the heart of Gamma II, Greater Noida — committed to delivering quality, compassionate healthcare to every patient, every day of the week.

Whether it's a sudden fever, a long-standing chronic condition, a child's health concern or just a routine check-up, Dr. Intjar Khan provides thorough, unhurried consultations with genuine care for each patient's wellbeing.

The clinic is open from 9 AM to 10 PM every single day — because health doesn't follow a schedule, and neither do we.

"Good medicine is not about the prescription — it's about the patient. Understanding their life, their worries, and their health together."
